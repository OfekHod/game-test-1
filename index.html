Lane — POC v3

build v154

3 heroes per side. Hero level and every stat run 1–26. Kills scatter XP orbs on the ground — nobody is handed experience, you have to go and pick it up, and the first hero to reach an orb keeps it. Spend the points in the strip above the HUD — hover or tap a stat or ability to see what it does. Your tank pushes the top lane and your support the bottom one on their own, advancing with their wave rather than ahead of it; drag a hero to a spot to detach him and post him there, and hit Assemble (or 0) to pull the squad back onto the hero you are controlling. Trees block movement and line of sight; your heroes, creeps and tower share one pool of vision. Match: two lanes fan out of one base per side and converge on the enemy base, with an outpost tower guarding each lane. Every 20 seconds each lane sends a wave — one big creep leading three smaller ones. Creeps chase whatever enemy comes close, so you can pull a wave off its lane — but only so far before it turns back. Three neutral camps sit in the woods between the lanes, soft and respawning 5 seconds after you clear them; the ground past the lanes is loose cover only. Neutral camps hide in the tree rings between the lanes and respawn, so they are worth circling back to. Your heroes auto-attack the nearest enemy on their own, holding back enough mana for a skill — aim and fire yourself for more. Destroy the enemy base to win.

Tutorial: its own map — the same shape as a Match but bare: one river to wade, one treeline to walk around, one camp, and nothing else in the way. Eighteen steps, one thing each: walking, shooting, the blink, a fight for the orbs it drops, levels, your squad and the combination that needs two of them, then orders, the forest, the lanes and the towers. It ends by putting you straight into a real Match.

Survival: no enemy base and no enemy heroes — just your tower alone in the arena, with mobs closing in from every side in growing numbers. Your squad sticks together by default. Score is how long you last.

Desktop: 1/2/3 or Tab to switch hero, WASD move, mouse to aim, click to fire, space for skill.
Phone: drag anywhere to move, tap where you want to shoot, tap the circle for your skill.

Points decide it if no base falls. Forest mob 1 · enemy creep 2 · big creep 3 · enemy hero 30 · tower 200. Destroy the enemy base and you win outright.
